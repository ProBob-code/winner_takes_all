## Why they pass

| reason | buyers | in their words |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| habit | 6 | "The money is nothing, 1,500 a month I would not even notice. But I run my competition night on a whiteboard now and it works, and I am the one who will end up standing there fiddling with a phone instead of working the floor." (P007) · "The price is nothing and the disputed-frame problem is real, but it is not my signature on the subscription and my floor staff will not touch new software on a busy Tuesday. They will go back to the whiteboard the first time the screen lags and I will be paying for something nobody opens." (P008) |
| other | 3 | "I run the floor, I don't sign up for things - the owner does, and ₹1,500 is small but it is still his money and his decision. And honestly it solves the fight over a frame, not the empty Tuesday; it assumes I already have a league night running, and I don't." (P011) · "I run the floor, I do not sign for a monthly bill in the owner's name, however small it is. And I have never organised a league night, so I would be buying a scoreboard for an event that does not exist yet." (P019) |
| trust | 1 | "I do not own the room. I run my league in whoever's hall will have us that week, on whoever's wifi, so a screen on the wall and a per-venue price is not really sold to me. And I have had a rep on the phone about software every other week for years; I am not going to put my Tuesday night on an app I have never seen hold up." (P005) |

## Why they buy

| reason | buyers | in their words |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| need | 9 | "The whiteboard works inside one room, but across my venues I have no idea who is leading anything after week two, and the notebook is wherever the last manager left it. For 1,500 free for a month I will put it on the pool night at my busiest branch and see if the league table actually brings the same faces back; the other branches stay on paper until it proves itself." (P001) · "Fifteen hundred a month is less than one night's till, and the free month means I risk nothing, so I will put it in one venue and watch. The part I actually want is the screen settling a disputed frame, because right now that argument lands in my WhatsApp group at eleven at night and I am the referee." (P002) |
| convenience | 1 | "Fifteen hundred a month is less than I lose in one argument over a frame, and the screen is already on the wall playing cricket nobody is watching. I'll take the free month because the league table is the part I can't do on a whiteboard - people coming back on a Tuesday to defend a place is my slow night fixed." (P014) |

## What would flip a no

- Show me it keeps running with no signal at all - scores still go in on the phone and sync up later - and send someone to sit with me and my two helpers for the first night so none of us has to learn it from a manual.
- Someone comes to my venue, sets it up on my screen themselves, and runs one night standing next to my floor staff so I see them do it without me touching anything.
- If you set up the first night yourself, in my venue, and showed one of my floor staff running the bracket end to end without me standing over him, I would take it to the owner the same week.
- Show me it runs fully offline on my own WiFi or off a phone hotspot, with scoring that keeps working and syncs later, and that the bracket can share the screen with the cricket instead of taking it over.
- Show me a pool hall my size where the Tuesday count actually went up and stayed up for two or three months after they put this in, with their numbers, not a demo.
- Show me two or three venues like mine in India with real before-and-after footfall on their competition night, plus a simple week-by-week guide to starting one from nothing - then I take it to the owner myself and run the free month.
- If it worked for someone like me with no venue - bracket and league table on the players' own phones, no screen needed - and I could run a whole night without touching it after setup, I'd try the free month.
- A venue my size, in a city like mine, showing me its counter takings for the competition night before and after - if the night actually got bigger, I would put it up next Tuesday.
- Give me one page I can put in front of the owner: what a first league night actually looks like, who gets the entry money, who funds the prize, and what it did to a comparable hall's Tuesday takings. If he says yes to that I will run the free month.
- Show me it keeps running on the venue's own screen with the connection pulled - bracket and scores still working offline, syncing later - and I will take the free month to the owner myself.

Buyers say they would buy **0.9 times** in the first month on average.
