# Winner Takes All — run your competition night

Software for your venue to run its own tournament night. You set up a bracket in
about a minute, your regulars join from their phones by scanning a code, and the
live bracket and running scores go up on the screen on your wall.

It keeps score for 8-ball pool and for football, so a disputed frame is settled
by what is on the screen rather than by argument. Run it as a weekly series and
it keeps a league table across the weeks, so the same people come back next
Tuesday to defend their place.

₹1,500 a month per venue. No contract, cancel any month.

Players pay you nothing through the software and stake nothing on the app. Entry
is free, or you collect it at the counter in your own name, exactly as you do
now. Any prize is yours or a sponsor's to give. The app never touches your
customers' money, which is what keeps it clear of the 2025 online money gaming
rules.

First month free. If your regulars do not come back for a second week, you do
not pay.
